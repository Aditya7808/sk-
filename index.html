Our Services

What We Do

From a single room refresh to a full building exterior, we handle every painting and finishing need with care and precision.

Residential Painting

Full interior and exterior painting for flats, bungalows, and row houses. We cover living rooms, bedrooms, kitchens, bathrooms — every corner of your home, done right.

Commercial & Office Painting

Professional finishes for offices, shops, showrooms, and commercial buildings. We work around your schedule to minimise disruption to your business.

Exterior Painting

Weather-resistant exterior coats that protect your building and keep it looking fresh for years. We prep surfaces properly to prevent peeling and cracking.

Waterproofing

Stop leaks and dampness before they damage your walls. We apply waterproofing solutions for terraces, bathrooms, external walls, and basements.

Texture & Design Walls

Add character to your walls with textured finishes, POP designs, stencil patterns, and decorative wall art. We create feature walls that become conversation starters.

Wood Polishing & Painting

Restore the beauty of doors, window frames, furniture, and wooden panels with professional polishing, melamine coating, and enamel painting.
