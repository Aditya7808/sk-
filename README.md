## 📌 About

**SY Painting Hadapsar** is a professional painting company serving Mohammadwadi, Hadapsar, and the wider Pune area. This is their official business website, featuring a full portfolio, service listing, and a contact form that directly sends enquiries to WhatsApp.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🏠 **Responsive Design** — Fully mobile-friendly layout
- 🖼️ **Gallery** — Showcase of real completed projects
- 📋 **Services Section** — Residential, Commercial, Exterior, Waterproofing, Texture & Wood Polishing
- 📞 **WhatsApp Integration** — Contact form submits directly to WhatsApp
- ⭐ **Stats & Testimonials** — Animated counters and customer reviews
- 📍 **Google Maps Embed** — Business location visible on site
- ⚡ **Fast & Lightweight** — Pure HTML, CSS, JavaScript — no frameworks

## 📬 Contact Form → WhatsApp

When a visitor fills out the contact form:
1. Validation checks name, phone (10-digit Indian number), and message
2. Form data is formatted into a WhatsApp message
3. Browser redirects to `wa.me/919839170720` with the pre-filled message
4. The customer just taps **Send** in WhatsApp

## 🚀 Deployment (GitHub Pages)

1. Go to your repo → **Settings → Pages**
2. Source: **`main` branch → `/ (root)`**
3. Click **Save**
4. Your site will be live at: `https://Aditya7808.github.io/sk-/`
